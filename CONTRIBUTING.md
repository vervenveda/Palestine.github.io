# Contributing to the Palestine Historical Repository

Contributions should help readers verify evidence and understand context.

## Accepted contributions

- Primary-source records with stable links or catalogue identifiers.
- Research chapter improvements with citations.
- Corrections to dates, quotations, translations, or contextual statements.
- Carefully documented maps, photographs, and datasets with clear rights.
- Bibliographies, glossaries, and explanations of competing scholarly interpretations.

## Submit a research claim

Include:
1. Exact claim wording and the time/place it concerns.
2. Primary source, with page, paragraph, catalogue ID, or image reference.
3. A short explanation of what the source does and does not establish.
4. Independent corroboration, where available.
5. Relevant counter-evidence or scholarly disagreement.
6. Date checked and contributor's note on uncertainty.

## Editorial rules

- Avoid unsourced claims and fabricated quotations.
- Distinguish a source's wording from your interpretation.
- Do not project modern identities or borders unchanged into earlier eras.
- Do not equate a region, people, nation, government, and sovereign state without explanation.
- Attribute responsibility to specific actors and documented events; avoid collective blame.
- Use respectful language for all communities.
- Do not publish private personal information or identifying details about private individuals without a legitimate public-interest basis and permission.
- Respect copyright and licenses.

## Corrections

Open an issue or pull request with the original wording, proposed correction, evidence, and reason. Substantive changes should be recorded in `updates/changelog.md`. Do not silently erase a correction history.
