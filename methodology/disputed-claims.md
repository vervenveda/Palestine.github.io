## Initial questions to investigate

### Q-001 — How did the geographical meaning of “Palestine” change?
Distinguish ancient Greek usage, Roman administrative usage, medieval geography, Ottoman-era usage, Mandatory Palestine, and modern political terminology. Do not treat all uses as identical.

### Q-002 — What forms of political authority existed in the region before modern statehood?
Document the actual institutions and territories of each period. Avoid retroactively describing every region as a modern nation-state.

### Q-003 — How should Palestinian population continuity be described?
Examine archaeological, genetic, linguistic, documentary, and oral-history evidence with attention to what each method can and cannot show. Do not turn complex population history into a single uninterrupted or total-replacement narrative.

### Q-004 — What were the causes and local patterns of Palestinian displacement in 1947–1949?
Build locality-specific records from contemporaneous documents, military records, oral testimony, and scholarship. Avoid applying one cause to every location.

### Q-005 — What is the legal status of the occupied Palestinian territory?
Use the actual text of relevant treaties, UN resolutions, and court opinions. Distinguish legal conclusions about territory occupied in 1967 from questions not decided by those documents.
