# Sources and Research Gateways

This list prioritizes original issuing institutions, academic research projects, and archival guides. A source's institutional status tells us who produced it; it does not make every interpretation complete or neutral. Record access dates and verify individual documents before citing them.

## United Nations and international law

- **UNISPAL — UN Information System on the Question of Palestine:** https://www.un.org/unispal/data-collection/  
  Collection of UN documents, resolutions, maps, reports, and historical studies.
- **History of the Question of Palestine:** https://www.un.org/unispal/history/
- **Origins and Evolution of the Palestine Problem:** https://www.un.org/unispal/history2/origins-and-evolution-of-the-palestine-problem/
- **The Palestine Question: A Brief History (1980):** https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-206581/  
  An accessible institutional overview; it is not a substitute for direct ancient evidence.
- **International Court of Justice:** https://www.icj-cij.org/  
  Use official case pages and the full text of opinions or judgments.
- **UN OCHA occupied Palestinian territory:** https://www.ochaopt.org/  
  Dated humanitarian reporting and maps.
- **UNRWA:** https://www.unrwa.org/  
  Institutional material about Palestine refugees and agency operations.
- **OHCHR:** https://www.ohchr.org/
- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — State of Palestine:** https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/ps

## Ancient history and inscriptions

- **Inscriptions from the Persian Period to the Islamic Conquest:** https://www.inscriptionsisraelpalestine.org/  
  Academic corpus of inscriptions with images, translations, and contextual information.
- **Brown University — Inscriptions of Israel/Palestine:** https://cds.lib.brown.edu/cds-project/inscriptions-israelpalestine
- **Herodotus, *Histories*, Fordham Ancient History Sourcebook:** https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/ancient/herodotus-history.asp  
  A translated text gateway. Confirm quotations against a critical edition and cite book/section numbers.

## Archives and primary-source guides

- **UC Berkeley Library — Historical Primary Sources: Israel/Palestine:** https://guides.lib.berkeley.edu/c.php?g=810896&p=7333572  
  Includes pointers to British Mandate records, Ottoman legal materials, the Palestine Gazette, and oral-history collections.
- **UN General Assembly Resolution 181 (II), 1947:** https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-185393/
- **UN General Assembly Resolution 67/19, 2012:** https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-184195/

## Scholarly article on the name Palestine

- David M. Jacobson, “Palestine and Israel,” *Bulletin of the American Schools of Oriental Research* (1999): https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/1357617  
  Consult the full article and its references; distinguish the author's argument from primary attestations.

## Source checking

For every source, capture:
1. Authoring institution or author.
2. Document title, date, and version.
3. Stable URL or catalogue identifier.
4. Date accessed.
5. Exact page, paragraph, table, or inscription ID supporting the claim.
6. Whether it is a primary source, later scholarly interpretation, official legal record, or contemporary reporting.
7. Limitations, disagreement, or relevant correction notices.
